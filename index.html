Teacher Panel

Three Tenses Quiz · Power Up Level 6, Unit 5 · Class 6B · 50 questions · 100 points · 25 minutes · Deliberately harder than the single-tense quizzes.

Lesson Aim

To assess students' ability to choose between the past simple, present perfect and past perfect — not just form them — using time clues, results now, and the order of two past actions.

Learning Objectives

  • Choose the past simple for finished-time actions (yesterday, last week, in 1968, at 8 o'clock) and form it accurately, including irregulars (saw, broke, got, met, knew).
  • Choose the present perfect for actions connected to now — just / already / yet / ever / never / since / for, unfinished time (this month) and results now — with have/has + past participle (has worked, have left, has broken).
  • Choose the past perfect for the FIRST of two past actions, signalled by before, after, when, by the time, because — had + past participle (had finished, had already gone, hadn't slept).
  • In two-gap sentences (Part 3), combine tenses correctly in one sentence, and spot traps: have seen + yesterday, didn't went, has just ate, had see.

Desired Learner Outcomes

  • By the end of the quiz, learners can score across all five parts with answers sent to the class sheet (tab: ThreeTensesQuiz).
  • Learners can explain, from the review screen, why each answer takes its tense — naming the clue (finished time / result now / since-for / FIRST action).
  • Learners below 60 revisit the "How to choose" page of the Three Tenses guide before the next lesson; learners below 40 re-teach all three tenses in order.

Running notes

  1. Before: "Type your full name, then tap Begin. The timer starts straight away — 25 minutes for 50 questions, about 30 seconds each. This one is harder — take your time and read the whole sentence."
  2. Say: "Two kinds of questions. TAP — tap one answer. WRITE — type only the verb form, not the whole sentence. Spelling must be correct; capital letters don't matter."
  3. Key questions to say aloud: "Ask three questions: (1) Is there a finished time? → past simple. (2) Is it connected to now — just, already, yet, ever, never, since, for? → present perfect. (3) Are there TWO past actions? The FIRST one → past perfect."
  4. Warn about Part 3: "Part 3 has TWO gaps. You must get BOTH verbs right to score — check each gap separately."
  5. Suggested timing: Part 1 ≈ 4 min · Part 2 ≈ 5 min · Part 3 ≈ 5 min · Part 4 ≈ 5 min · Part 5 ≈ 4 min · 2 min to check.
  6. During: The top bar shows the countdown and how many questions are answered — no live score is shown to students.
  7. Finishing: "Finish & mark" warns about unanswered questions and jumps to the first gap. At 0:00 the quiz auto-submits; blanks count as wrong.
  8. After: Scores land in the sheet tab ThreeTensesQuiz (parts /20, total /100, time used). Walk through the review screen together — every question shows the correct answer and a one-line reason.
  9. Next student: Use "Reset attempt" on the results screen to return to the name gate.

"Reveal answers" highlights the correct option on every TAP question and shows the key under every WRITE question — use it on the projector after marking, or before the quiz to preview.
